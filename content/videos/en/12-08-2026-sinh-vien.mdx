One in the morning. The room is dark and the only light in it is a phone screen throwing
pale rectangles onto the wall. He scrolls one post into the next, and every one is the
same shape, the same rhythm, the same voice. Different words, identical point: the field
he studied is about to stop needing him. He is in his final year.

![Moment 1](/characters/12-08-2026-sinh-vien-1.png)

Three weeks go by like that. Four years of textbooks sit in the corner of the desk with
a thin layer of dust on them by now. It isn't that he's lazy — he's busy. Busy reading
about becoming unnecessary. Then one night he watches the clip that scared him most all
the way to the end instead of swiping away. The last frame is a price card and a bright
sign-up button. The person who frightened him for three weeks is selling something. Not
one of them will sit down and study a single page on his behalf.

![Moment 2](/characters/12-08-2026-sinh-vien-2.png)

Class is no different. One remark from the lecturer and the room buzzes for the rest of
the session. Out in the courtyard everyone has a take, everyone is worried — and worrying
together is far more comfortable than worrying alone, because it feels like preparation.
Except nobody is preparing anything. The one at the back who said nothing all session is
still there in the evening with his notes open. He already has a job, and nobody noticed.

![Moment 3](/characters/12-08-2026-sinh-vien-3.png)

That night he sits down and tries something, instead of worrying about it one more time.
The repetitive part he hands off entirely — and yes, it's fast, much faster than he is.
What's left is the hard part: understanding the problem, fitting the pieces into something
that actually runs. He sketches a diagram by hand, and there's one box he doesn't know how
to fill. School never got that far. So the frightening thing was never the fast tool. It
was that empty box. And an empty box is something you can write in.

> That's the part it can't reach.
> So I keep going, one day at a time.

Nobody took those three weeks from him. He handed them over, to people who make a living
from other people's fear. How many evenings have you handed over this week?

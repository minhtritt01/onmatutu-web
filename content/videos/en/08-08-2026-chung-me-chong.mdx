Twenty-six, and exactly one month into living in her husband's family home. The fear wasn't
something her mother-in-law had earned — she carried it in with her, assembled out of other
people's stories about people she had never met. And her mother-in-law barely spoke. No scrutiny,
no questions. Just watching.

![Moment 1](/characters/08-08-2026-chung-4.png)

Four days sick, four days off work, a short paycheck that month. The hard part wasn't running out
of money. It was not being able to ask. Asking would have meant admitting she was a burden in a
house she had only just entered. She typed a message and left it unsent. Then she pulled open a
drawer and found an envelope with money inside, left there at some point she couldn't pin down.
Nobody had said anything. The silence she had read as coldness turned out to be the way it was
given.

![Moment 2](/characters/08-08-2026-chung-5.png)

Every dinner that month was silent. She ate fast to get it over with, and in a whole month she had
told her mother-in-law nothing about herself. It was her husband who mentioned it later, casually,
the way you mention old household news: when his mother first came to this house, she was just as
afraid of *her* mother-in-law. That silent table wasn't one woman judging another. It was two
women who had sat in the same chair, decades apart.

![Moment 3](/characters/08-08-2026-chung-6.png)

She wanted to say thank you and couldn't find a way in. Then she learned her husband didn't know
about the envelope either. His own mother had hidden it from him. Which meant there was no one to
give her credit, no witness, and no debt to settle — she had designed it that way. The slip of
paper tucked inside said only: *don't feel awkward about it.*

> I hadn't managed to call her "mẹ" and mean it.
> She had already decided I was her daughter.

Some people love you in a way that leaves you owing nothing. It is a hard kind to notice, because
it never announces itself. Is someone in your family loving you like that right now, without your
having seen it yet?

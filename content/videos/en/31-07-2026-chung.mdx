Midnight, the lights already off, just the screen throwing a pale blue over his face. He meant to look for two minutes and then sleep. Those two minutes ran until one in the morning.

![Moment 1](/characters/31-07-2026-chung-1.png)

An old classmate just made manager. Another one moved to Japan. Another posted a photo in front of a dealership with a new car, everyone smiling. He doesn't resent any of them — that's the uncomfortable part. There's nobody to blame, only a low hum that says the whole world is moving and he's standing still somewhere without a name. A full hour of scrolling and not one moment of being glad for anyone. It was only when he set the phone down that he understood: he hadn't been reading about his friends at all. He'd been measuring himself.

![Moment 2](/characters/31-07-2026-chung-2.png)

Then he remembered something. Last week he posted a photo too — a coffee in a nice café, good light, the kind of picture that looks like a person doing fine. That was the afternoon he'd just been chewed out, sitting alone at a table for two with the other chair empty the whole time. The photo doesn't tell that part. It isn't a lie; it simply doesn't say. And he knew that, knew it the moment he hit post — and still, looking at everyone else's photos, he believed every single one.

![Moment 3](/characters/31-07-2026-chung-3.png)

One in the morning, still scrolling. But this time a sentence arrived clearly: he was holding his worst day up against their best one. His bad day he lived through minute by minute; their good day he got in one frame, already chosen, already cropped, already colour-graded. Losing that comparison isn't surprising, and losing it doesn't mean anything either. He turned the phone face down on the table.

> My life is the raw version. Theirs is edited.
> Of course I lose. Just sleep.

The next morning he still got up, still went to work, and his friends were still there, still moving faster. Only the ruler he'd been holding at midnight got put down along with the phone.

And you — that last photo you posted, what did it leave out?

Twenty-three, a decent degree in a drawer, and a folder on her laptop holding more than forty CVs. She counted them once, then stopped counting. The silence from employers she had gotten used to. What she hadn't gotten used to was someone asking what she wanted to do, and finding nothing to say.

![Moment 1](/characters/11-08-2026-chung-1.png)

Every night the same: open the laptop, scroll the list, every line the same shade of grey. Nothing marked, because nothing came back. Then one afternoon she cleared out her bookshelf — not looking for anything, just tired of the mess. Every book she pulled down had a folded corner, an underlined sentence, and the same subject running through it: what people think, what they're afraid of, why they end up the way they do. She sat there among them a while. It turned out she did care about something. Nobody had ever named it out loud — including her.

![Moment 2](/characters/11-08-2026-chung-2.png)

The three months before that, the alarm rang and went quiet every morning. Her diploma still leaned against the wall, never hung. The curtains stayed shut all day; every square on the wall calendar stayed blank. People called her lazy. She almost believed it. But her shoes sat by the door, toes pointed out, ready for a long time already — she wasn't short on energy. She was short on somewhere to go.

![Moment 3](/characters/11-08-2026-chung-3.png)

First session, she sat at the back, still holding a phone with an unanswered question on it. Then she looked up. Twenty people, all of them just arrived, none of them sure, all of them feeling their way. The board at the front was still blank — first day, nobody has written anything yet. When it ended, she added her name to the last line of the sign-up sheet, for session two.

> Twenty people, nobody sure.
> She isn't lost. Just slower.

She still doesn't know what she'll be doing a year from now. But the next morning, for the first time in three months, something was waiting for her at eight. And you — the thing you keep coming back to without ever naming it, what is it?
